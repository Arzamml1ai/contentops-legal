ContentOps Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026

Who this applies to

ContentOps is a private, single-user application operated by its owner to manage the owner's own YouTube channels and Instagram accounts. It has no other users and does not collect data from the public.

Data we access

When the owner connects an account and grants permission, ContentOps may access:

  • From Google / YouTube: basic channel information (name and picture), permission to upload videos and set their details, and permission to read and reply to comments on the owner's own channel.
  • From Meta / Instagram (through a linked Facebook Page): the account name, permission to publish posts and Reels, and to read and reply to comments on the owner's own account.

How the data is used

Access is used only to publish and schedule the owner's own content and to manage comments on the owner's own accounts, as directed by the owner. Data is not used for advertising or sold, and it is not used to train any machine-learning model.

Storage and sharing

Access tokens and the owner's content are stored in the owner's own private Supabase project, protected by access rules. Data is not shared with, or sold to, any third party, except that content is sent to YouTube and Instagram when the owner publishes it.

Google API Services User Data Policy

ContentOps's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept only while the accounts are connected. The owner can disconnect an account in the app at any time, which deletes its stored tokens, and can revoke access in the Google Account permissions page or in Facebook / Instagram app settings.
